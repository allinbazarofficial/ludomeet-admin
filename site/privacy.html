LudoMeet Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

This policy explains what information the LudoMeet app ("LudoMeet", "we") collects, how we use it and the choices you have. LudoMeet is intended for users aged 18 and over.

Information we collect

  • Account information — when you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address and Google profile photo, and we give you a LudoMeet player ID.
  • Profile information you choose to add — display name, profile photos, bio, gender, birth date and country.
  • Game and wallet data — coins, diamonds, level, game statistics, purchases, withdrawal requests (including the wallet address or Binance ID you enter) and partner deposits (including the payment screenshot you upload).
  • Messages — text, photos and voice messages you send to other players, and the time they were sent and read.
  • Social data — who you follow, who follows you, profile visits, players you block and reports you submit.
  • Microphone — used only while you hold the record button to send a voice message. We do not record at any other time.
  • Photos — only the pictures you choose to upload (profile, chat, reports, payment proof). Saving a chat photo stores it in your phone's gallery.
  • Online status — whether you are online and when you were last active, which other players can see.
  • Technical data — device type and app version, used to keep the service working and to prevent fraud and abuse.

How we use information

  • To run your account, save your progress and show your profile to other players.
  • To deliver messages, process purchases, deposits and withdrawals.
  • To keep the community safe: for safety and to handle reports, the LudoMeet team may review messages, photos and profiles, and may warn, mute or ban accounts that break our rules.
  • To prevent fraud, multiple accounts and cheating, and to improve the app.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Your data is stored with Google Firebase (Google Cloud). Purchases are processed by Google Play. Other players can see your public profile (name, photos, player ID, level, country, gender and age, bio, followers) and the messages you send them. We may disclose information if required by law.

Data retention

We keep your data while your account is active. When you delete your account we delete your profile, photos and account data within 30 days, except records we must keep for legal, fraud-prevention or financial reasons (for example payment and withdrawal records), which are kept for up to 2 years.

Your choices

  • Edit your profile, photos and bio in the app at any time.
  • Block players and report abuse from their profile or chat.
  • Delete messages for yourself, or for everyone within 2 minutes of sending.
  • Delete your account in the app: Profile → Delete account, or see playludomeet.com/delete-account.html.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and protected by access rules so that only you, the people you talk to and the LudoMeet team can see private information.

Children

LudoMeet is not intended for anyone under 18. If we learn that a child is using LudoMeet, we will delete the account.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
