LudoMeet Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Information we collect

If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile photo from Google. We also store your in-game data such as coins, diamonds, game statistics, the profile name, photo, bio and country you choose, and any withdrawal requests you submit (including the wallet address or Binance ID you enter).

How we use it

We use this information to run your account, save your progress, process purchases and withdrawals, prevent fraud and improve the app. Offline games can be played without an account.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Data is stored with Google Firebase. Payments are processed by Google Play.

Your choices

You can edit your profile in the app, sign out at any time, and request deletion of your account and data by emailing allinbazarofficial@gmail.com.

Children

LudoMeet is not intended for children under 13.
