Delete your LudoMeet account

This page explains how to delete your LudoMeet account (developer: MF Corporation) and the data linked to it.

Option 1 — in the app

  1. Open LudoMeet and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile and scroll down.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Option 2 — by email

Send an email to allinbazarofficial@gmail.com with the subject Delete my LudoMeet account, from the Gmail address you use in LudoMeet, and include your LudoMeet player ID.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, photos, bio, gender, birth date, country and player ID.
  • Your coins, diamonds, level and game statistics.
  • Your follows, followers, visitors and block list.
  • Your messages are removed from your side; messages already delivered may remain visible to the people you sent them to until they delete them.

What we keep and for how long

Payment, deposit and withdrawal records and reports involving your account are kept for up to 2 years for legal, fraud-prevention and accounting reasons, then deleted. Everything else is deleted within 30 days of your request.

Unused coins and diamonds are lost when an account is deleted and cannot be restored.
